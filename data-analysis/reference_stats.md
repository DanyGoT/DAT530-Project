Generated from `receive.csv`, `order.csv`, `delivery.csv`. Data runs Feb 2020 – Oct 2026; **2026 is partial (to 2026-10-22)**.

## Cleaning

- Rows kept: receive 7935/8629, order 7833/8557, delivery 4662/6182.
- Dropped: dates after today, missing/zero quantity, equipment not a pipe type (catches shifted rows), orders where machining/HB/scrap > joints received, negative durations.
- Merged scope labels: Re_Inspection → Re Inspection, R2R → RtoR.
- Dates used are all ISO `YYYY-MM-DD`. Only `Receive_complete_Date` has mixed formats (6 values) and it is not used.
- Order receive date = `Returned_Date` looked up via `Receive_No`. Order year = year received (else year inspected).
- Delivered + scrapped joints are only ~60% of joints received (43–73% depending on year, see section 4); delivery volumes are under-recorded. Use them for timing and batch size, not totals.
